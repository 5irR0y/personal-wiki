## Role summary

Lead a team of SREs who own availability, reliability and performance. The manager owns the team's share of availability targets, leads incident and problem management, builds telemetry, and is the escalation point for urgent customer issues. They are the bridge between engineering, operations and senior leadership. AI use is a theme throughout: for triage, root cause analysis, toil reduction and monitoring.
