### Wyalusing State Park *(optional — Sunday only, unlikely)*

**13081 State Park Ln, Bagley, WI 53801**  
*(Mississippi + Wisconsin river confluence; ~6 mi south of Prairie du Chien)*

- **If we go:** Sunday on the way home, **after Cave of the Mounds** — only if time allows
- **Likelihood:** Probably **not enough time** with House on the Rock + Cave + lunch + long drive home
- **Hours:** daily **6 AM – 11 PM** (year-round)
- **Admission:** WI state park vehicle sticker required — daily ~$8 (non-resident may differ) or annual $28/$38
- **Phone:** (608) 996-2261
- **What it is:** 500-ft bluff overlooks, river confluence views, 14 mi hiking trails, small caves (Sand Cave, Pictured Rock), Native American mounds, picnic areas, playgrounds, canoe/kayak on backwaters (rentals in season)
- **Family-friendly highlights:** Point Lookout / Henneger Point vistas; Sugar Maple Nature Trail + Pictured Rock Cave; Sentinel Ridge (views; loop section stroller-friendly); Sand Cave Trail
- **Rough drive times (verify in Maps):**
  - Cave of the Mounds → park: ~1.5 hr (estimate)
  - Park → HuHot La Crosse: ~1 hr 15 min
- **If we did stop:** La Crosse HuHot fits the route better than Rochester
