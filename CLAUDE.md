# Personal Wiki — AI operating instructions

Leroy Dehnke's life / non-work knowledge base: personal to-dos, trips and plans, media watch
history, resume and career material. See `README.md` for the folder map.

This repo is standalone. It must work when cloned on its own, with no work wiki beside it.

@.cursor/rules/personal-wiki.mdc

## Filing rules

Before creating or editing any note, read `ai-guidance/repository-rules.md`. It covers the inbox,
archiving projects, to-do ids (`Pers-###`), and trip-checklist handling.

## Hard rules

- **Do not invent facts.** If something is unclear, ask, or file under the best fit with a short
  note marking the uncertainty.
- **Dates are absolute.** Never "next Tuesday" — write `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- **Everything here is personal by default.** Work-adjacent content (resume, work history, career
  notes, some notes about co-workers) is allowed. Day-to-day work records (meetings, team
  to-dos, 1:1 topics, the company contact directory) belong in the separate work wiki
  (`personal-work-wiki` repo), not here.
